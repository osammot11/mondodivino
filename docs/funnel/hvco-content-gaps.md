## Editorial divergence

The questionnaire calls the real guide **Hidden Italian Gems**. The approved funnel master and all implemented delivery copy call it **Italy Beyond the Famous Labels**. This revision preserves the explicit project-wide editorial decision to avoid breaking message continuity. Resolve the definitive title before updating the PDF, email template, landing metadata and guide mockup together.

## Publication gate

Before paid traffic, place the final PDF in the review scope and verify every landing bullet against an exact page/section. If a promise cannot be mapped to real content, reduce the landing promise rather than fabricating material.
