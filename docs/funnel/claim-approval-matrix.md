# HVCO USA — Claim approval matrix

Internal working document. Do not publish or expose through a public route.

Status legend:

- `APPROVED`: usable in the prudent formulation recorded here.
- `PROVISIONAL`: fact supplied for this revision, but avoid expanding it into an absolute.
- `REQUIRES_CLIENT_CONFIRMATION`: operational detail needs an explicit decision.
- `REQUIRES_EVIDENCE`: potentially usable only after obtaining publishable proof.
- `DO_NOT_USE`: excluded from current copy.
